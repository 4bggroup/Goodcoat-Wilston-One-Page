07 / Before the first brushstroke

A few good
questions.

Getting the details clear makes the whole project feel easier.

Can we stay home while the painting is done?

Often a repaint can be planned around occupied rooms, but access, ventilation and the coating system matter. Discuss who uses the space, pets and any sensitivities before agreeing the work sequence.

We haven’t chosen colours. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Bring a few ideas, photographs or a favourite material. Narrow the palette first, then test physical samples in the room before confirming the final paint colour.

What should a painting quote include?

Look for the areas and surfaces included, the agreed preparation, the coating system, access requirements and exclusions. Make sure timing, payment terms and any change to the scope are clear before work starts.

What about older Queenslanders?

Older coatings and surfaces need an assessment before preparation begins. Tell the painter about the age of the home, previous coatings, peeling paint or known hazardous materials so the right preparation approach can be discussed.

How long will the work take?

It depends on the size of the job, surface condition, access, drying intervals and weather for exterior work. A useful timeframe follows a clear scope and a look at the property.

Can you work around our business hours?

Include trading hours, access restrictions and any sensitive equipment in the enquiry. Working hours and staging should be discussed and confirmed as part of the project plan.
